## Off-site steps (these matter as much as the site)
1. Verify the site in Google Search Console, submit /sitemap.xml, set `NEXT_PUBLIC_GSC_VERIFICATION`.
2. Create a Google Business Profile as a service-area business (hide the address if you have no public premises). Use the same name, phone and website.
3. Ask real patients for genuine Google reviews. Never buy or fake them.
4. Use the same name, phone and website on Instagram, LinkedIn and any directories.
5. Add genuine content over time (for example "When to call a doctor at home") instead of many thin pages.
